## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: one polished logo concept presentation for SentinelX, a dark-mode Linux infrastructure monitoring, observability and protection SaaS for DevOps engineers. Create an actual clean logo image, not a website, not UI, not a product mockup.

Primary request: a sophisticated, memorable, minimal, original identity with youthful contemporary design intelligence. Evolve the idea of a shield into a distinctive abstract X-shaped guardian emblem. The references in the conversation show a simple blue outline shield and a tiny triangular shield favicon; treat those as brand-context cues, not designs to copy.

Design: one compact emblem whose strong outer silhouette gently suggests a shield, made from just two broad, precisely balanced folded geometric bands. An open, deliberate negative-space diagonal crossing makes the letter X discoverable in the center. Slightly softened corners, controlled curvature, clean optical alignment, a calm upright stance. Bold enough for a 16–24px favicon, not a thin outline shield containing a separate X. The shape must feel unified, restrained and ownable, not like clip-art, a medieval crest, a gaming/esports badge or a generic cybersecurity stock logo. Favor simple readable geometry over intricate interweaving. It should still read as one cohesive symbol in monochrome.

Color: predominantly a single flat soft electric periwinkle blue close to #8FAAFF; a restrained small icy-cyan flat facet may distinguish one of the two bands. Absolutely no gradients, no glow, no 3D bevels. Background a perfectly plain uniform near-black #080808, matching the SentinelX operational dashboard.

Composition: a single centered vertical lockup, emblem above and wordmark below, on a landscape 3:2 presentation canvas with ample intentional negative space. Emblem occupies about a third of the canvas height, with balanced visual weight. Wordmark smaller than emblem, crisp warm-white modern geometric sans-serif, medium weight, carefully kerned, no excessive tracking. Text verbatim and only: "SentinelX" (capital S and final capital X). No slogans, captions, grid lines, explanatory copy, side-by-side options, frames, extra icons, decorative scenery or watermark. Flat vector-like graphic precision with immaculate edges; premium independent design-studio quality.
